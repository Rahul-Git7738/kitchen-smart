Mumbai’s coastal climate causes standard MDF and particleboard to absorb humidity, swell, and harbour mould. BWP (Boiling Water Proof) IS:710 Marine Plywood is bonded with phenolic resin, guaranteeing 100% resistance to moisture, direct water spills, and termites.
