Meet Mr. Ashraf Shaikh

Founder & Principal Craftsman of Kitchen Smart India — Championing precision German ergonomics, honest factory pricing, and personal supervision for every Mumbai home.

Mr. Ashraf Shaikh Founder & Design Director
The Vision Behind The Craft

"A Kitchen Should Be Built to Last Generations, Not Just Look Good on Day One."

With over 10 years dedicated to interior architecture and modular kitchen engineering, Mr. Ashraf Shaikh founded Kitchen Smart with a singular commitment: eliminating the headaches, delays, and compromises that homeowners routinely endure with unorganized carpenters.

"In a fast-paced city like Mumbai, your kitchen must endure intense daily cooking, heavy spices, moisture, and high humidity," explains Mr. Shaikh. "That is why we refuse to use commercial MDF or particleboard for kitchen carcasses. We build strictly with IS:710 Marine Grade BWP Plywood, cut to millimeter precision on German HOMAG CNC machinery, and fit them with authentic Blum and Häfele motion hardware."

Under Mr. Ashraf Shaikh's direct leadership, Kitchen Smart has grown from a boutique workshop into one of Mumbai's most trusted modular kitchen brands, earning a flawless 5.0-star reputation on Google with projects completed across Lalbaug, Mumbai Central, Worli, Marine Lines, Bandra, Andheri, and Mira Road.

Our 5-Step Flawless Execution Process

From initial laser measurement to final sparkle handover, here is how Mr. Ashraf Shaikh and his team bring your dream kitchen to life.

01

Consultation & Space Planning

We examine your kitchen dimensions, utility locations, cooking habits, and storage requirements to map out the ideal golden work triangle (Hob, Sink, Refrigerator).

02

Photorealistic 3D Design

Our architectural design team generates realistic 3D visualizations so you can see your exact colors, quartz countertops, LED vitrines, and cabinet finishes before spending a rupee.

03

Transparent Factory Estimate

Receive a crystal-clear itemized quote detailing exact hardware specs, drawer systems, and materials. Zero hidden charges, zero surprise escalation fees.

04

German CNC Precision Factory Fabrication

Every cabinet carcass is pre-cut, edge-banded with waterproof PUR glue, and pre-drilled on automated German HOMAG CNC machinery to ensure 0.1mm accuracy.

05

4 to 7 Days Rapid Fitout & 10-Yr Handover

Pre-assembled modular units arrive at your doorstep and are installed quietly and cleanly in 4 to 7 working days by our in-house certified fitters, backed by a written 10-Year Warranty.
