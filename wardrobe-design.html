Wardrobe Questions

Frequently Asked Wardrobe Questions

Everything you need to know about custom wardrobes from Kitchen Smart India.

Sliding wardrobes (such as Aristo systems) move horizontally along tracks, requiring zero forward swing clearance—ideal for tighter Mumbai bedrooms. Hinged wardrobes open outward 90° to 110°, allowing full view of all compartments simultaneously when room space permits.

We offer velvet jewelry and watch trays, pull-out trouser racks, tie/belt carousels, hydraulic pull-down wardrobe hangers for high lofts, biometric digital security drawers, and deep wire basket hampers.

Carcasses are built with calibrated marine-grade or moisture-resistant engineered core. Shutters are available in European acrylic, PU matte lacquer, tinted toughened glass in anodized aluminum frames, natural wood veneers, or textured leatherette.

We route recessed aluminum profile channels into the cabinet sides or hanger rods. Infrared or magnetic door sensors automatically trigger the lights when a shutter slides or swings open, operating on low-voltage 12V/24V drivers.

Pricing depends on overall square footage, shutter finish, and hardware complexity. High-end sliding glass and lacquered wardrobes typically start around ₹1.5 Lakhs to ₹5+ Lakhs. We provide an exact itemized quotation after measuring your room.

After finalizing the 3D drawings and measurements, factory fabrication takes 15 to 20 working days. Installation in your home is completed in just 2 to 4 days with zero sawdust mess.
