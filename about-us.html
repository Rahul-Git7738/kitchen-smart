Our Heritage & Passion

Crafting Timeless Luxury Kitchens for Modern Mumbai Lifestyles

At Kitchen Smart India, we believe the kitchen is the true heart of the home. Founded and actively led by Mr. Ashraf Shaikh, our team has transformed over 700+ residences across South Mumbai, Western Suburbs, Thane, and Navi Mumbai into culinary masterworks.

Unlike retail aggregators who outsource your kitchen to third-party contractors, Kitchen Smart manages every stage under one roof. From photorealistic 3D visualization and precision German CNC factory cutting to 4-to-7 day dust-free on-site assembly, our master craftsmen deliver flawless fit and finish.

We exclusively partner with global leaders including Blum (Austria), Häfele (Germany), and Hettich, pairing certified European motion hardware with 100% Boiling-Water-Proof (BWP IS:710) Marine Plywood to guarantee decades of seamless durability in Mumbai's humid climate.
